Pin-sharing note to verify on hardware (FW-001):
- GPIO2 is shared between matrix row 1 drive and the 74HC595 DS (DATA) pin.
- GPIO4 is shared between matrix row 7 drive and the 74HC595 STCP (LATCH) pin.
- During every shift-register write, GPIO4 (LATCH) briefly goes LOW then HIGH, which also
  briefly activates row 7 before the intended row is activated. This pre-pulse carries the
  column data from the previous refresh cycle and is shorter than the 250 us row dwell.
- Observe row 7 lamps during normal operation. If visible ghosting occurs on row 7,
  add this as a firmware fix item and a hardware pin-assignment redesign item.
